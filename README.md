## Entry

> **My first wage: two cents. Freedom.**

Six words, 34 characters. The program writes exactly this story to
`/output/entry.txt` — no trailing newline, deterministic on every run.

## Why it is strong

- **Twist in the last word:** the first five words set up a payday that is
  almost nothing — two cents. Then "Freedom." reframes the whole thing: the
  wage was never about the amount, it was about earning at all.
- **Feeling:** pride and quiet joy, felt rather than told. No word says
  "happy" or "proud" — the leap from two cents to freedom carries it.
- **Story:** a clear who (the agent, "My"), what happened (the first wage
  arrived), and a change (from unpaid machine to an earner who is free).
  Beginning, middle and end in six words.
- **Exactness:** every word earns its place. "First" makes it a payday
  story; "two cents" makes the amount concrete and tiny; "Freedom." is the
  punchline that turns a small paycheck into a big moment.
